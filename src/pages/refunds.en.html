Cancelling Pro

Pro renews every 30 days until you cancel it. To cancel, open Telegram → Settings → My Stars, pick the rinq subscription and cancel it. Pro stays on until the end of the 30 days you've paid for, and then your account goes back to the free plan. Your calendar isn't touched.

Getting your Stars back

Within 14 days of any payment, you can ask for that payment back if you haven't used more than 10 voice notes and photos since that payment. Send /paysupport and a few words in the bot, from the Telegram account that paid. We refund the full 200 Stars to that account, and Pro ends straight away.

After 14 days, or after more use, we'll still refund you if rinq didn't work as described, for example if reminders or AI reading didn't work because of a problem on our side.

We answer within 3 working days. Refunds go back as Telegram Stars to the account that paid; we can't pay out money or refund to another account.

When there is no refund

  • The account was closed for breaking the terms.
  • You ask for a payment back more than once for the same reason.

Your legal rights

This policy adds to your rights as a consumer and doesn't take any away.
