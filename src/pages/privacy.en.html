rinq is a calendar for Windows, Android and Telegram. This policy says what rinq keeps about you, why, where, who else sees it, for how long, and how to get it removed. rinq is run by {{ operatorHtml }}, {{ countryHtml }} ("we"). Write to {{ emailHtml }} about anything here.

What we keep and why

WhatWhy
Your email address and password. The password is stored only as a hash; we can't read it.To sign you in on your devices.

Voice notes and photos are not kept. When you send one, rinq passes it to the services below to turn it into plans, then drops it. Only the resulting plans are saved, and only if you keep them.

rinq has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and we don't sell or share your data for marketing.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, calendar and settings: until you delete your account.
  • A plan you delete: removed for good 60 days later, so your other devices have time to catch up.
  • The monthly count of voice notes and photos: 13 months.
  • The voice-note length log: 30 days.
  • Reviews and complaints from the website: until you ask us to remove them. We can take a review off the page at any time.
  • Your Pro plan and its payment reference: until you delete your account. Telegram keeps its own record of every payment.

Deleting your account

In the PC or phone app, open Settings and choose Delete my account (on the PC it is on the Account tab). This removes your account, calendar, settings, Telegram link and usage counts at once, and stops a Pro subscription from renewing. The copy of the calendar on the device you used stays there until you remove the app. If you can't sign in any more, send /paysupport delete my account to the bot, or write to {{ emailHtml }} from the email you signed up with.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it or delete it, object to how we use it, or ask us to stop using it. Write to {{ emailHtml }}. We'll answer within 30 days. If you're in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

rinq is for people aged 16 and older. We don't knowingly keep data about anyone younger. If you think a child has an account, write to us and we'll delete it.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies, keeps nothing in your browser and loads nothing from other sites. When you send a review or a complaint, or open the reviews, the page talks to our database (Supabase) and nothing else. The apps don't use cookies either.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each account can reach only its own data, and the database enforces this for every request. No system is perfectly safe; if something goes wrong that puts your data at risk, we'll tell you.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the apps and the bot will say so before the change applies.
